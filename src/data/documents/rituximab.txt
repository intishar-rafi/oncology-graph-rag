Rituximab is a monoclonal antibody directed against CD20, a surface protein found on B lymphocytes. It is a cornerstone therapy for B-cell malignancies including diffuse large B-cell lymphoma, chronic lymphocytic leukemia, and other forms of non-Hodgkin lymphoma. By binding CD20, rituximab triggers several mechanisms of immune-mediated cell killing, including complement activation and antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity, leading to depletion of both malignant and healthy B cells. Rituximab is frequently combined with standard chemotherapy regimens to improve response rates.
