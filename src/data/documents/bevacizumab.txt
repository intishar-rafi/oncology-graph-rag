Bevacizumab is a monoclonal antibody that targets vascular endothelial growth factor A (VEGFA), a signaling protein that tumors secrete to stimulate the growth of new blood vessels, a process called angiogenesis. By neutralizing VEGFA, bevacizumab starves tumors of the blood supply they need to grow beyond a small size and metastasize. It is used across a range of solid tumors including metastatic colorectal cancer, renal cell carcinoma, and pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma, typically in combination with cytotoxic chemotherapy.
