Vemurafenib is a targeted therapy for melanoma, the most aggressive form of skin cancer, specifically for tumors carrying the BRAF V600E mutation, which is present in roughly half of all melanoma cases. The mutated BRAF protein drives continuous signaling through the MAPK pathway, promoting uncontrolled cell growth. Vemurafenib selectively inhibits this mutant kinase, producing rapid tumor shrinkage in many patients. It is often used together with a MEK inhibitor to delay the onset of resistance, which commonly develops within a year of monotherapy.
