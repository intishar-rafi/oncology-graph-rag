Pembrolizumab is an immune checkpoint inhibitor that targets PD-1 (programmed cell death protein 1), a receptor expressed on T cells that tumors exploit to evade immune attack. By blocking the interaction between PD-1 and its ligand PD-L1, pembrolizumab restores the ability of T cells to recognize and destroy cancer cells. It has demonstrated broad efficacy across many cancer types, including advanced melanoma, non-small cell lung cancer with high PD-L1 expression, and head and neck squamous cell carcinoma. Pembrolizumab was among the first checkpoint inhibitors to receive a tumor-agnostic approval, based on molecular biomarkers rather than tumor location alone.
