Imatinib revolutionized the treatment of chronic myeloid leukemia (CML), a blood cancer caused by the BCR-ABL1 fusion gene, which arises from a translocation between chromosomes 9 and 22 known as the Philadelphia chromosome. Imatinib is a small-molecule tyrosine kinase inhibitor that binds the ATP pocket of the BCR-ABL1 kinase, blocking its constitutive activity and halting the proliferation of leukemic cells. Beyond CML, imatinib also inhibits the KIT and PDGFRA kinases, making it effective in gastrointestinal stromal tumors (GIST), a sarcoma of the digestive tract frequently driven by mutations in those same genes.
