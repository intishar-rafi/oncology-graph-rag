Osimertinib is a third-generation EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor developed for patients with non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) whose tumors carry activating mutations in the EGFR gene. Unlike earlier EGFR inhibitors, osimertinib remains effective against tumors that have developed the T790M resistance mutation, a common cause of treatment failure with first-generation drugs like erlotinib. By selectively inhibiting mutant EGFR while sparing the wild-type receptor, osimertinib achieves strong tumor control with a more favorable side-effect profile. It has become a first-line standard of care for EGFR-mutated NSCLC.
