Alectinib is a second-generation ALK inhibitor developed for non-small cell lung cancer driven by rearrangements of the ALK gene, which occur in roughly 3-5% of NSCLC cases, typically in younger patients who have never smoked. Alectinib was designed to overcome resistance mutations that emerge during treatment with the first-generation ALK inhibitor crizotinib, and it also penetrates the blood-brain barrier more effectively, making it particularly valuable for controlling brain metastases, a common complication of ALK-positive lung cancer.
