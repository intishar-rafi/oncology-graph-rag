Olaparib is a PARP inhibitor that exploits a concept called synthetic lethality in cancers with mutations in the BRCA1 or BRCA2 genes. PARP1 is an enzyme involved in repairing single-strand DNA breaks; in cells that already have impaired double-strand break repair due to BRCA mutations, blocking PARP1 leads to an accumulation of DNA damage that the cell cannot survive. This makes olaparib particularly effective in BRCA-mutated ovarian cancer, where it is used as maintenance therapy after chemotherapy, and it has also shown activity in HER2-negative breast cancer patients carrying germline BRCA mutations.
