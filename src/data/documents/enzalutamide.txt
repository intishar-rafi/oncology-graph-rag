Enzalutamide is an androgen receptor signaling inhibitor used in the treatment of prostate cancer, a disease that is heavily driven by androgen receptor activity. Unlike older anti-androgens, enzalutamide binds the androgen receptor with high affinity and blocks its translocation into the nucleus, preventing it from activating the transcription of genes that drive tumor growth. It is approved for use in metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer, where the disease continues to progress despite low testosterone levels, indicating that the androgen receptor pathway remains active through alternative mechanisms.
